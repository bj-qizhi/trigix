# Acceptable use

Use Trigix only on systems, accounts, applications, data, and user sessions that you own or are explicitly authorized to operate.

You must not use Trigix to:

- access another Tenant, user session, account, or Device without permission;
- bypass authentication, Approval, operating-system trust, protected fields, access controls, rate limits, or safety policy;
- collect credentials, biometric data, voice, screenshots, personal data, or confidential content without a lawful purpose and appropriate notice;
- distribute malware, exploit payloads, spam, fraud, harassment, deceptive impersonation, or unlawful surveillance;
- interfere with availability, integrity, audit records, update trust, or incident response;
- automate decisions or actions where applicable law, contract, or policy requires qualified human review;
- test third-party systems through Trigix without the owner's explicit permission;
- present a development-signed artifact, unverified mirror, or modified release as an official Trigix production build.

Deployment operators must define allowed integrations and actions, use least privilege, protect credentials, review logs and retention, keep supported versions, and give users a visible way to stop local automation and voice capture.

Good-faith security research performed under [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md) is handled through that policy. Suspected abuse that exposes a vulnerability or release-integrity risk should be reported privately. Other public project conduct is governed by the [Code of Conduct](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
